About

I am an Assistant Professor at the Centre for Advanced Financial Research and Learning (CAFRAL), an organization promoted by the Reserve Bank of India.

I obtained my PhD in Economics from Brown University in 2023. My research sits at the intersection of macroeconomics, monetary policy, and the formation and propagation of expectations — with a focus on how central banks can communicate effectively and anchor beliefs in the economy.
