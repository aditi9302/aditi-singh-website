About

I am an Assistant Professor at the Centre for Advanced Financial Research and Learning (CAFRAL), an organization promoted by the Reserve Bank of India. I am currently posted in the Monetary Policy Department of the Reserve Bank of India.

I obtained my PhD in Economics from Brown University in 2023. My research has two main strands: the first examines how monetary policy affects real economic outcomes, particularly household consumption, while the second studies how expectations are formed and how they shape the transmission of monetary policy.
